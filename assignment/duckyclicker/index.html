About Ducky Clicker

Click the duck. Get more ducks. Buy buildings. Get even more ducks.

A cookie-clicker style incremental game, built on the Ducky Game API. Save progress is stored locally and synced to your DuckMath account.

Play more games at duckmath.org

Tips:

  • Buildings produce ducks automatically, even when you're away.
  • Buy enough of a building to unlock upgrades that double its output.
  • Golden Ducks pop up periodically — click them for big bonuses!
  • Earn achievements to passively boost production (+1% each).
  • Ascend to reset with permanent Prestige Feathers.
  • Click the 🪶 Feathers chip to open the Feather Shop — spend Feathers on permanent perks that survive every ascension.
  • Spend dabloons in the Ducky Shop for boosts and perks.
